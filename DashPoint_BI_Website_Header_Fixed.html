GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about pricing, subscriptions and getting your reporting up and running.

How does DashPoint pricing work?

You pay a once-off implementation fee and a required monthly managed BI subscription from R495. Any necessary third-party software, licences, connectors or dedicated hosting are additional. We confirm the full costs before you approve the project.

Is the monthly subscription compulsory?

Yes. Every DashPoint solution requires an active managed subscription. Essential starts at R495 per month and covers simple spreadsheet reporting, agreed refresh checks and limited support. More complex solutions require a higher plan.

What if my business needs Power BI?

Power BI solutions start on our Professional subscription from R995 per month, excluding Microsoft licensing. Private report sharing generally requires suitable licences for each viewer and report creator, unless an eligible capacity or existing licence covers them. We check your existing subscriptions before recommending any purchases.

Do I need to pay for Looker Studio?

Standard Looker Studio can be used without a separate platform subscription. If your solution needs paid connectors, premium features or other software, we identify these costs before implementation.

What are paid data connectors?

Connectors retrieve data from business systems such as accounting or CRM software. Not every solution needs one. If a paid connector is required, we confirm compatibility, refresh frequency and subscription cost before you approve it. The fee is additional to your DashPoint subscription.

Who pays for licences, connectors and hosting?

These costs are not included in the advertised DashPoint management fees. Normally you purchase the required subscriptions in your business account and we configure and manage the agreed connections. Consolidated billing may be available only where supplier arrangements permit it. Your quotation identifies each cost and who bills it.

What does my monthly subscription cover?

Agreed dashboard maintenance, refresh monitoring and technical support within your plan. Professional starts at R995, Connected at R1,995 and Enterprise at R3,500 per month. New dashboards, major changes, extensive data cleanup and additional integrations are quoted separately.

What happens if I cancel?

Your managed service, monitoring and support end in accordance with your agreement. Your underlying business data remains yours. Continued report access, handover, notice periods and any outstanding third-party subscription commitments are agreed in writing before implementation.

Do I need to replace my existing business software?

Usually not. We work with suitable spreadsheets, exports and supported connections from your existing systems. We assess your data and access requirements first, and recommend the simplest suitable approach.

How often will my dashboard update?

This depends on your source system, connection method and software plan. We agree a refresh schedule before implementation. Some solutions still need a manual export or data entry; real-time updates are not assumed.
